## Izlaz

Primary request: one simple sculpted open doorway with a small outward-pointing exit arrow, shown nearly front-on. Style: premium matte 3D Soft Clay matching an Easter-themed mobile game: warm porcelain ivory, muted thematic lavender, tiny sage accent, deep plum separation; rounded hand-sculpted edges, shallow relief, soft upper-left studio light, clean ambient occlusion. Composition: single compact centered silhouette, the door and arrow large and legible with minimal transparent padding. Genuinely transparent background and alpha. No square button, no badge backing, no scene, no text, no numbers, no glossy plastic, no orange/red/cyan, no flowers, eggs, sparkles, metallic details, or watermark.

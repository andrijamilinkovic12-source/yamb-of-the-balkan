# Green Soft Clay — Quarterly League logo

The production logo was generated with built-in ImageGen as a transparent 3D Soft Clay PNG.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: transparent high-quality 3D Soft Clay logo for the Quarterly League of the Yamb of the Balkan mobile game, also used as a subtle watermark on the main league card. Create one centered diamond / rhombus emblem with controlled, mostly straight architectural sides and softly rounded corners. Build a deep muted forest-green matte-clay central field, a thick warm limestone-ivory raised outer rim, four very small restrained terracotta clay corner fasteners, and a faint low-relief six-column Yamb score-sheet grid embossed into the green field behind the lettering. Exact text only: a smaller line "YotB" centered above very large letters "QL". Render the exact casing Y-o-t-B and Q-L; no other words or characters. Make the lettering chunky, rounded and warm ivory, with the small YotB line carrying a subtle terracotta inset shadow. Premium handcrafted polymer clay, subtle tactile grain, crisp separation, clean ambient occlusion, soft upper-left studio lighting, high-end 3D clay neumorphism, readable at 80 px. Use the supplied green icon only as a material, palette and lighting reference; invent an original emblem design and do not reproduce another theme's logo ornaments. True transparent background around the rhombus. No square button backing, card, landscape, flowers, Easter colors, desert colors, blue/cyan/purple, metal, gloss, extra icons, crown, trophy, dice, additional text or watermark baked into the PNG.

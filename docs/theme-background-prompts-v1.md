# Pozadine devet tema — prvi autorski renderi

**Arhivirani, odbačeni smer.** Korisnik je prekinuo ovu seriju rendera i zatražio da se prethodne pozadine zanemare. Nijedan od ovih promptova nije važeće uputstvo za narednu generaciju. Vaskršnji render je samo mogući izuzetak za naknadni pregled, bez odobrenja. Novi promptovi moraju poštovati oba univerzalna pravila iz `theme-definitions.json`: **bez kiča i nakićenosti** i **živopisan, prepoznatljiv predeo**. Naročito se odbacuje podvodno morsko dno za Plavi Okean.

Datum: 2026-10-06. Svaki prompt je bio zaseban novi render. Zajednički zahtev ove odbačene serije: mobilna portretna kompozicija 9:16, 3D Soft Neomorphism, vrh i sredina mirni za logo, kartice i Yamb tablu, detalji pretežno uz rubove i pri dnu, bez teksta, logotipa, UI kartica, okvira, vodenog žiga i preuzimanja motiva druge teme.

## Svetlo Zlato — Light Gold

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: mobile game portrait background. Create an original 3D Soft Neomorphism environment in Soft Satin / Brushed Pearl direction. A luminous champagne-gold and warm ivory space made of broad satin folds and pearl-smooth architectural curves, with a few restrained brushed-gold edge details and tiny pearl glints near the lower corners. Refined, airy, premium, not a clay landscape. Keep the central 65% quiet and low-contrast for dark readable game UI; avoid a hard horizon. Soft diffuse studio light. Palette centered on #D8B466, #FFF4D6 and #F3DFAC. Portrait 9:16. No words, letters, logos, dice, game board, controls, human figures or watermark.

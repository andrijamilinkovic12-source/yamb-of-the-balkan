# Dragon projection

Two original RGBA masters from the built-in image generator. Run `build.py` (Pillow and NumPy) to produce the mobile WebP assets in `www/assets/effects/dragon-projection-v2`. The face keeps its original square framing so the mouth anchor is invariant: centre x=.5, y=.65, jaw band from .55 to .78. The plume atlas holds four individually feathered 320px tiles.

## Face prompt

Use case stylized-concept. One premium transparent RGBA game VFX sprite for Yamb of the Balkan: the head only of an ancient Balkan fantasy dragon materializing as a translucent engraved jade and pale cyan light hologram. Camera straight on, both eyes, TWO swept horns, symmetrical snout facing the viewer, looking slightly down at us. Jaws open in a well-defined oval roar, an obvious deep dark open cavity beneath the nostrils, upper and lower fangs around it; the cavity must remain DARK and empty to composite animated fire FROM INSIDE IT later. Eyes warm pale amber. Expressive, sculptural beveled scales with ethereal luminous contour ridges, restrained and legible as a small watermark at 135 pixels, elegant not frightening. Shape fades at the bottom just below the chin, no long neck. Light teal sea-glass shading and pale pearly edges, avoid solid bulky skin, avoid excessive tiny lines and noise. Fully visible silhouette and horns, frontal compact square composition with ~7% transparent margin. Only the head. NO fire, smoke, weapons, aura disc, interface, symbols, text, frame, scenic background or ground. Genuine transparent background, not black.

## Plume prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. A single physically rendered fire VFX sprite atlas on a truly transparent background for a mobile fantasy game. Square texture divided conceptually into 4 equal quadrants; precisely ONE isolated small curling mass of flaming gas in each quadrant, no grid lines. The four masses are successive turbulent shapes of HOT FIRE looking into the lens, foreshortened billowing flame lobes, as if a fantasy dragon breathes towards the camera. Top left short dense white-gold ignition curl surrounded by orange tongues; top right bright amber rolling flame with asymmetric forked curl; bottom left large turbulent copper-orange fire with crescent yellow-hot edges, pockets of real transparency; bottom right thinning orange-red flame wisps breaking apart, visibly different silhouette. Dimmer translucent warm body and bright edges, fine optical falloff toward alpha zero, well exposed fire and rounded depth. All shapes are irregular, NOT four balls, rings, circles or toruses; NO mushroom clouds, no smoke blobs, no campfire logs, no directional jet, no candles, no emissive line art. No scenery, no illustrations of dragons, NO text, no baked background or glow rectangle. Each shape centered in its own quadrant with visibly EMPTY transparent borders between neighboring cells; all edges fully included. Mobile VFX designed to overlap and scale during animation.

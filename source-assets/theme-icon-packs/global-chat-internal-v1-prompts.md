# Global Chat interior PNG specification

Generated as separate transparent-background bitmap assets. Each illustration uses one clear 3D Soft Neomorphism silhouette, the theme's own Clay or Smooth Rubber / Matte Plastic material, palette, and icon DNA. No text, frame, pedestal, confetti, decorative sparkle, photorealism, or recoloring of another theme's asset. The empty/loading role is a speech bubble with three clearly spaced dots; the send role is a small paper-plane action symbol. Source masters and per-theme manifests are under `{theme}/global-chat-internal-v1/`; 384 × 384 RGBA deliveries are under `www/assets/theme-packs/{theme}/canonical/global-chat-internal/`.

# Svetlo Zlato — Tournament PNG prompt set

Method: built-in imagegen. Ten missing room roles were made with separate prompts. The three tournament medals were then regenerated from the approved Light Gold champion trophy: one original gold medal, followed by silver and bronze rank edits that preserve its silhouette. The first two completed-match drafts were discarded; the compact olive diamond is final.

Common specification: original isolated 3D Soft Neomorphism PNG, Smooth Rubber / Matte Plastic, broad softly inflated forms, quiet diagonal cuts, warm upper-left light, matte honey gold `#E6BE83`, warm ivory `#FFF1D7`, muted olive `#76612B`, warm brown `#875024`, genuinely transparent square canvas, readable on mobile. No scene, text, glitter, metallic shine, filigree, unnecessary ornament or copied Green silhouette.

Production normalization: each accepted 1254 × 1254 RGBA master is resized to a distinct 256 × 256 RGBA runtime PNG by `scripts/build-tournament-room-pack.ps1`; mapping and SHA-256 values are stored in `manifest.json`. Existing turnirske medalje with a different cup were replaced, while their canonical paths remain stable for every consumer.

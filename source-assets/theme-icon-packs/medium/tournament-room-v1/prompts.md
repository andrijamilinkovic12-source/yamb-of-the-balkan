# Trula Višnja — Tournament PNG prompt set

Method: built-in imagegen, one role per call. The approved Trula Višnja champion cup was the reference for the silver finalist. The approved icon DNA and the first generated cherry-clay info icon guided the remaining roles. The register seal was the reference for its unregister and locked siblings. No asset was recolored from another theme.

Common specification: 3D Soft Neomorphism, **Clay** direction, slightly asymmetric hand-molded mass with one broad depression, matte volumetric shadows, cherry body `#8B3038`, deep shadow `#431720`, warm cream `#F1C3B2` and restrained peach `#E39A70`. One readable symbol on a genuinely transparent square PNG, no ornamental detail, photorealism, metal shine, text or copied Green/Light silhouettes.

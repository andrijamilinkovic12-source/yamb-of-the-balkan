# Live effect components v1

Built-in image_gen. Runtime files are cropped to transparent bounds and resized without palette changes.

## trumpet

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent PNG VFX prop for a tasteful Balkan wedding celebration in a mobile board game. One actual brass trumpet only, side three-quarter view, bell pointing right and slightly up, complete recognizable realistic instrument with three piston valves, mouthpiece and curved tubing. 3D Soft smooth matte material, softly rounded craft, restrained warm antique brass and ivory reflections. Clean isolated cutout, generous transparent margin, no pedestal, no notes, no ribbons, no text, no particles, no decoration. Readable at 90px. This physical brass instrument is a universal component reused across themed lighting.

## frost-corner

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent PNG VFX component: delicate frost growing inward from the TOP LEFT CORNER of a rectangular game board. Square image, the outer left and top edges carry a thin milky blue ice crust. Fine translucent feathered ice veins extend diagonally toward center, tapering away and fading to fully transparent toward the lower right. Ice has smooth soft 3D volume, cool white and pale blue, subtle refraction, light and refined, no coarse rock, no huge rounded ice chunks, no snow pile, no opaque square background, no frame visible on right/bottom. A real icy corner texture to mirror into all four corners of any rectangular board, not a finished frozen board. No text, no glitter, no decorative snowflakes.

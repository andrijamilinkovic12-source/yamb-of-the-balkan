🎯 Objective

The goal is to score as many points as possible by rolling 6 dice and combining the obtained values into the table. You have 3 rolls per turn.

📊 Columns

  • ↓ DOWNWARDS: Populated sequentially from number 1 down to Yamb. Cannot be skipped.
  • ↑ UPWARDS: Populated sequentially from Yamb up to number 1.
  • ⇅ MIDDLE: Populated from the middle towards the ends (from MAX and MIN fields towards 1 and Yamb).
  • S FREE: Can be populated in any order during the game.
  • H HAND: Populated exclusively after the FIRST dice roll.
  • 📢 CALL: You must explicitly call the desired field immediately after the first roll.

🏆 Scoring and Sections

SECTION 1 (Fields 1 to 6)

Only matching numbers are summed. If the sum in this section is ≥ 60, you receive a bonus of +30 points.

SECTION 2 (MIN - MAX)

Calculated by the formula: (Max - Min) * Number of ones (from the first section). If the result of this calculation is ≥ 60, you receive a bonus of +40 points.

SECTION 3 (COMBINATIONS)

  • THREE OF A KIND (3 of a kind): Brings the sum of dice + 20 points bonus.
  • FULL HOUSE (3 of a kind + 2 of a kind): Brings the sum of dice + 30 points bonus.
    *Note: 5 identical dice (Yamb) can also be entered as a Full House, as the game automatically recognizes it and treats it as 3 and 2 of the same dice.
  • POKER (4 of a kind): Brings the sum of dice + 40 points bonus.
  • YAMB (5 of a kind): Brings the sum of dice + 50 points bonus.
  • STRAIGHT (5 in a row): Represents a sequence of 5 consecutive numbers (1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 2, 3, 4, 5, 6). Scored depending on which roll it was obtained: 1st roll is 66, 2nd roll is 56, and 3rd roll is 46 points.

📊 Stat Tracking

Yamb of the Balkan records your every move. Here's what we track in the statistics section:

📈 Basic Stats

  • ⚡ Power Index (PI): The main indicator of your success that analyzes all your results and skills.
  • 🏆 Record: Your personal best score ever achieved in any game mode.
  • 🎮 Total Games: The sum of all matches played across all game modes.
  • ⚖️ Win/Loss Ratio (W/L Ratio): Shows your overall efficiency in direct clashes. This statistic exclusively records matches in online Random duels, duels with friends, Tournaments, as well as the local Two-Player mode. Note: If the opponent leaves the game or their time expires (Anti-Troll timer), you are automatically awarded a win, but only in online modes. In local mode, a win is recorded only if the game is properly finished.
  • 🔥 Win Streak: The number of consecutive wins achieved in online duels and tournaments.
  • 📈 Average: Average number of points per game based on all matches played.
  • 🏅 Trophies: A collection of special achievements you have unlocked.
  • 💰 Balance (Ducats): Displays the current amount of earned ducats you have.
  • 🌟 All-Time PTS: Total number of points you have earned since installing the game.
  • ⚔️ Rival: The friend you have crossed dice with the most times.

⚔️ Head-to-Head Duels (H2H)

Who is really better? This section provides a detailed overview of the clash history with specific players. For every rival who becomes a friend, a special H2H card is formed:

  • 🏆 Biggest Win: Your highest achieved score (in points) against that specific rival.
  • 👑 Largest Margin: Shows the most dominant victory and the maximum point difference achieved against the rival.
  • 💔 Heaviest Defeat: The largest point difference by which this rival has ever defeated you.
  • 🔥 Win Streak: Your longest streak of consecutive wins achieved exclusively against this player.
  • 🎯 Your Average: The average number of points you achieve in matches with this specific rival.
  • ⚖️ Win/Loss Ratio: Displays your win/loss ratio with a specific rival expressed in percentages.

⚔️ Competitions

Besides standard matches and duels, Yamb of the Balkan also offers organized competitions for the most ambitious players.

🏆 TOURNAMENTS

  • Competition system: The weekly tournament consists of 8 players. It is played on a knockout basis: quarterfinals, semifinals, and the final. Each duel is played as a single match.
  • Registration and fee: Registration costs 2500 ducats. Refunds and cancellations are only possible while the player gathering phase lasts. Once all 8 players are registered, the tournament begins, and withdrawal is not possible.
  • Scheduling matches: Times are not fixed! Opponents in the bracket propose and accept the match time themselves through the app. Once both players confirm the time, the match can be started. The tournament can last a maximum of 7 days from the moment it starts.
  • Prizes: The winner of the entire tournament gets a huge Power Index (PI) boost and will be forever inscribed in the Hall of Fame, plus rich rewards in ducats!

🏅 QUARTERLY LEAGUE

  • League concept: The Quarterly League is a seasonal competition that lasts 3 months (a quarter). Every completed game (solo or online) brings points that are added up to determine your rank.
  • Ranks: You progress through ranks: Amateur, Pro, Master, Legend, and Titan. Promotion to a higher rank depends exclusively on the total number of points collected in the current quarter.
  • Prizes: At the end of each quarter, the invested effort pays off handsomely! The highest-ranked players win extremely valuable prizes in ducats, which they can use to further upgrade their profile.
  • End of season and Hall of Fame: Besides ducats, the top 3 players at the end of the cycle enter the Hall of Fame (winning medals), while the first-placed player takes the prestigious title of Cycle Champion. After that, points are archived in 'All-Time', and the new battle starts from zero.

💰 Ducats and Economy

Ducats are the main currency in Yamb of the Balkan. You need them to enter elite tournaments, as well as to buy all cosmetic add-ons in the game. There are many ways to earn them:

  • 🎲 Playing games: Each completed game (in any mode) brings you as many ducats as the points you scored. You can also double this earnings at the end of the match by watching a short AdMob sponsored ad!
  • 🎁 Daily Challenge: Try your luck every day! Before you are 6 dice – the first four are added up, the fifth die multiplies that sum, and the sixth die multiplies the entire previous result. With a little luck, this is an ideal opportunity for incredibly fast earnings!
  • 🎬 Treasury Rewards: Whenever you need extra ducats, enter the Treasury and watch an ad to immediately claim a bonus of 500 ducats.
  • 🏆 Tournaments and Quarterly League: The most persistent and best players win the most! The winners of the weekly Tournaments, as well as players who take the top three spots at the end of the Quarterly League cycle, are automatically rewarded with huge amounts of ducats for their success.

🖥️ Server Support and Security

Behind Yamb of the Balkan is a powerful and stable server designed to provide you with a flawless and fair multiplayer experience. Here is what our server controls and secures in the background:

  • 🛡️ Anti-Cheat and Fair Play: The server actively monitors every game, results, and your ducat balance, preventing manipulations and hacking attempts. Validation of rewards (like the Daily Challenge) is done directly on the server. An "Anti-Troll" timer is also built-in, which automatically awards you a win if the opponent intentionally delays their turn!
  • ☁️ Cloud Save and Sync: All your settings, statistics (including H2H history), trophies, and inventory from the Treasury are securely saved and instantly synced with the database.
  • ⚖️ Smart Moderation: The server uses an advanced filter to detect and penalize vulgarity in the global chat and usernames. Players who break the rules automatically receive restrictions, guaranteeing a healthy environment.
  • 🔌 Connection Stability (Grace Period): We are aware that mobile internet can be unstable. If your connection drops in the middle of a tense duel, the server activates a "Grace period" of 30 seconds – giving you time to reconnect and continue the game before awarding the win to your opponent.
  • 🏆 The Brains of the Operation: Manages Matchmaking, the friend request system, Quarterly League operations, Hall of Fame, and asynchronous Tournament scheduling.

🛠️ Server maintenance, optimization, and new security patches are done daily to keep the game completely stable, fair, and safe for all users!

5. Account deletion

Facebook sign-in does not require a Google account. You may voluntarily link Google and Facebook in the game Settings by verifying the relevant account. Both sign-in methods are then associated with the same Yamb profile identifier and provide access to the same Dukati, inventory, statistics and progress.

If you already have a Yamb profile, first sign in using its existing method, then choose to link the other account in Settings. Matching email addresses alone do not confirm that accounts are linked. If two separate Yamb profiles exist, their Dukati, inventory and progress are not merged automatically.

Permanently delete your account and associated data in the game settings or on the Account deletion page. Deletion also removes progress, Dukati and inventory without recovery. Your external sign-in account remains active. See the Privacy policy for processing and retention details.

If Google and Facebook are linked to the same Yamb profile, deletion covers the entire shared profile and its game data. Once deletion is complete, neither linked sign-in method provides access to the deleted profile. Your personal Google and Facebook accounts remain active. Signing out of the game or uninstalling the app does not request deletion of your Yamb profile.
